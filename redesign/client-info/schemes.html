vpn-ui / account info / five panels

Five panels, drawn full size

Every one carries the same content: identity, health, quota, expiry, subscription link and its QR, every membership's own artifact, and a way into More Information. They differ in shape, not in what they hold.

01

One Scan

the subscription is the product

Most customers need one thing: the subscription. So it is the whole top of the panel, QR at 176px, with JSON and Clash beside it. Health rides in a thin bar under it, and the six memberships are one line each until you want one. More Information opens inside the row.

Cost: lowest. One hero block, one row list, one expander.

02

Bento

everything at once, nothing nested

The panel's own mosaic language from redesign/bento.html. The subscription is the feature tile with its QR; quota, runway and health are their own tiles; each membership is a tile showing what it gives. Nothing is hidden behind anything. More Information flips a tile.

Cost: low, and it reuses a grid the panel already ships.

03

Ledger

a table, because the panel is tables

One band across the top holds identity, quota, runway and the subscription with a QR button. Below it the memberships are a real table: status, inbound, protocol, used, what it gives, actions. Sortable, scannable, and it does not get worse when an account has twenty inbounds. More Information expands the row.

Cost: low. It is an a-table, which every other page already is.

04

Dossier

the account is pinned, the list moves

A fixed left panel holds everything that is true of the account: who, health, quota, runway, and the subscription with its QR, always on screen. The right side is the memberships as an accordion, each opening to its own artifact. More Information is the second half of an opened item.

Cost: low-medium. Two columns, one accordion.

05

Carousel

one thing at a time, at full size

A filmstrip of thumbnails, and the subscription is the first frame. Whatever you pick fills the stage underneath at full size, so a QR is big enough to scan across a desk and a config is big enough to read. More Information is a toggle on the stage.

Cost: low. A strip, a stage, one selection.

06

Notes

The subscription is per account and covers 5 of 6 inbounds here: GRE has no subscription entry at all, and the credential VPNs appear in it only as a connection card that carries the endpoint and quota but cannot be dialled. Every panel above says so next to the link rather than claiming full coverage. Two memberships are deliberately broken in this fixture: IKEv2 has moved 0 B since it was created, and GRE is switched off on its own inbound while the account is enabled.
