The account is pinned on the left and never scrolls away: who, health, quota, runway, the subscription with its QR, and Export TXT / PDF for the whole account. The right side is the memberships, each with its own Export TXT / PDF in the row and its artifact inside.

Try: open any membership · the left column never moves · every row exports on its own
Both exports already exist; this is wiring, not new code. The per-inbound pair is exportClientTxt(dbInbound, client) and exportClientPdf(dbInbound, client) from ClientActionsMixin, the same two the icon row on a membership already calls. The account pair is exportTxt(accountTargets(row), row.email) and its PDF twin, already behind the account row's menu, where accountTargets() fans out over every membership so the file carries every protocol the customer was sold.
Two things the export block should say out loud. A whole-account export is not instant: buildCards() fetches the WireGuard, AmneziaWG and SSH configs per membership and IKEv2's Remote ID separately, so the button needs a spinner, not just a click. And GRE contributes a row with nothing to scan and no config, because its recipe is deliberately kept out of exports; the count under the buttons says 6 inbounds so nobody wonders why the file has one quiet entry.
