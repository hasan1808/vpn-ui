Endpoint, five ways

The first sketch printed panel.example.com:8443 as a third line in the identity cell. It answers the right question — a port on its own is half an address — and it has three problems worth designing away: it makes the row a line taller, it repeats the same hostname down the entire column, and on a fronted inbound the address it prints is not the one a client dials.

Each of these five gives up something different to fix those. The fixture now carries cdn-fronted, whose real address is a CDN hostname the panel has never been told, and cdn-ws, which is bound to one interface — the two rows that decide whether a scheme tells the truth.
