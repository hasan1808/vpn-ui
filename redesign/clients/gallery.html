Five ways to list accounts

The same twelve accounts, five information architectures. Everything is drawn in the panel's own tokens — teal accent, the four reserved status hues, neutral protocol chips — so what you are choosing between is the structure, not the paint. Pick one and it gets built; pick two and I will graft the better half of the loser onto the winner.
