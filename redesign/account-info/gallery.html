vpn-ui, account info modal

Five ways to show one account

What is being replaced is the account half of modals/inboundInfoModal: 799 lines that render an inbound's transport and a customer's identity, usage and links as one column of a-tag elements in <table> rows. It is opened per membership from the Clients expander, so the operator arrives already knowing the email, the quota, the expiry and the protocol. What they came for is the part the row cannot show.

Five information architectures, same account, same tokens. Each is drawn inside a plate the width of a real modal, with its body capped at 640px and scrolling inside, because that constraint decides more here than any styling does.

Sample data. One account on four inbounds of four families, so every shape the real modal has to render appears at once: a 36-character uuid (vless), a password credential (trojan, l2tp), an email-identity protocol with no credential to show and no share link at all (ssh), a membership switched off on its own (l2tp), a subscription URL too long for any of these boxes, and a share link of roughly 250 characters. The "Show depleted" control above swaps the same account into the state the depletion sweep leaves it in, so all five can be judged on the ugly case rather than the happy one.

Two figures are marked, not hidden. Xray counts per account with no inbound in the stat name, so the vless and trojan memberships cannot report bytes of their own: both show the same unattributed remainder, tagged. Their liveness has the same problem, which is why both carry the hollow lamp (connected somewhere in Xray) while ssh, which can account for itself, carries the filled one.
